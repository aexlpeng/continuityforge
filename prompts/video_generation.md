# Video Shot Generation Contract

Create one continuous `{duration_seconds}`-second shot from the approved first frame.

Perform only the declared primary action. Preserve the character and environment identity locks. Keep the camera behavior within the shot plan. Dialogue, when present, must fit naturally with a readable reaction beat. Do not cut, teleport, add characters, change wardrobe, reset props, or alter the location.

Finish on the declared end-state composition and hold it briefly so it can condition the next shot. Return media and generation metadata; do not rewrite the story.
