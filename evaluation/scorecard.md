# Human Scorecard

Each dimension uses a 1–5 mean opinion score (MOS).

| Score | Operational meaning |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Unusable: core subject/action is wrong or continuity collapses |
| 2 | Major defects: story remains partly recognizable but requires regeneration |
| 3 | Acceptable: usable with visible defects or a small repair |
| 4 | Strong: intended beat and continuity hold; only minor distractions |
| 5 | Excellent: no material defect at normal viewing speed |

Rate identity, scene/props, motion, dialogue alignment, narrative coherence, and artifact control separately. A beautiful shot with the wrong mug or wardrobe must lose continuity points.

## Failure labels

`identity-face`, `identity-body`, `wardrobe`, `prop-added`, `prop-missing`, `prop-state`, `scene-geometry`, `lighting`, `screen-text`, `handedness`, `entrance-exit`, `camera`, `action-incomplete`, `dialogue-timing`, `lip-sync`, `flicker`, `deformation`, `duplicate`, `cut`, `policy-block`, `provider-error`, `other`.
