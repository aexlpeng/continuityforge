# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in ContinuityForge welcoming and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, assume good faith, give actionable technical feedback, disclose limitations, and respect the rights and consent of people represented in media. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, doxxing, sexualized attention, threats, and knowingly publishing deceptive or non-consensual identity media.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior threatens a safe, constructive community. Report incidents privately to the maintainer contact listed in the repository profile. Maintainers will handle reports as confidentially as practical.

This compact policy is inspired by the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
